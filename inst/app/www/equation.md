#### Welfare function

### $Y_{ijt} = f(W_{jf(t)}, X_{ijt}, E_{jt}) + \epsilon_{ijt}$

- $Y_{ijt}$: outcome of individual/household $i$ in location $j$ at time $t$
- $W_{jf(t)}$: weather conditions in location $j$ at time $f(t)$
- $X_{ijt}$: characteristics of individual/household $i$ in location $j$ at time $t$
- $E_{jt}$: characteristics of location $j$ at time $t$
- $\epsilon_{ijt}$: error term
